The Foundation

Before Anything Else — The Truth Must Be Clear

Before United Ummah can call people towards preparation for Imam Mahdi, revival of Islam, and establishing Khilafah — we must be absolutely clear about one thing:

We cannot begin this mission by simply calling people to gather forces, form armies, build groups, learn fighting, pick up swords, start physical combat training, or rush into confrontation wherever oppression is happening.

That is not how real Islamic revival begins.

📖Before strength — comes guidance.
💡Before action — comes knowledge.
💎Before leadership — comes purification.
🛡️Before resistance — comes discipline.
🕌Before Khilafah — comes the people who are worthy of carrying Khilafah.

If we call untrained, emotional, confused, weak, undisciplined, and spiritually empty people towards major responsibilities — the result will not be revival. The result will be chaos.

The mission of Imam Mahdi, the revival of Islam, and the establishment of Khilafah cannot be carried by people who do not know the Quran, do not understand Salah, do not know the Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, do not understand Akhirah, do not understand Islamic responsibility, do not know the systems of Islam, and do not have discipline over themselves.
🕋Before we need people who can stand in the field — we need people who can stand in Salah.
💬Before we need people who can speak against oppression — we need people who can speak the truth against their own nafs.
🌍Before we need people who can defend the Ummah — we need people who understand what the Ummah is.
👑Before we need people who can talk about Khilafah — we need people who understand the Deen that Khilafah is meant to establish.
The First Step

The First Step Is to Build the Muslim Individual

This is why the first step of United Ummah is not physical training, not digital activism, not financial power, not public campaigns, not Masjid revival, and not political organization.

We need Muslims who are:

Whose hearts are connected with Allah.
Who can read the Quran correctly.
Who understand the purpose of life.
Who know the Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
Who understand the stories of the Prophets.
Who understand the Khulafa-e-Rashideen.
Who understand Salah with meaning.
Who understand Akhirah and accountability.
Who understand Islam as a complete way of life.
Who understand Khilafah with knowledge, not emotion.
Who are disciplined, sincere, patient, courageous, organized, and useful for the Ummah.
Only after this foundation can a person move towards the next responsibilities.

United Ummah is calling people to:

Not This
  • Chaos
  • Blind anger

  • Rushing without preparation
  • Untrained, confused people
This
  • Preparation
  • Disciplined strength

The Curriculum

The Six Stages of the Deen Foundation

The curriculum is structured across six progressive stages. Every student of United Ummah follows this roadmap according to their current level. Each stage builds upon the last — there is no shortcut and no jumping ahead.
1
Stage 1
Learning to Read the Quran
Children Teenagers Adults Reverts
▼

Stage 1 is for children, teenagers, adults and reverts who need to learn Quran reading from the beginning or improve their reading. The first and most important requirement for this stage is a teacher. The teacher may be a Madrassah teacher, online teacher or private teacher. Quran reading and Tajweed require correction, listening and guidance. In most cases, a student should not try to learn Quran reading completely alone. However, if a student cannot find or afford a teacher, the following resources can help as support material.

The goal of Stage 1 is that the student becomes able to read the Quran properly, confidently and beautifully.

2
Stage 2 — Runs parallel with Stage 1.3
Building Iman, Interest and Islamic Foundation
Children Teenagers Adults Reverts
▼

This is one of the most important stages of the curriculum. If a student reads the Quran but does not understand who revealed it, who received it, what message it carries, what the purpose of life is, and what will happen after death, then Quran reading can become a burden instead of a source of love and guidance.

For this reason, Stage 2 is designed to build the heart before heavier learning begins. This stage introduces students to:

  • Stories of the Prophets.
  • Seerah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
  • Day of Judgement.
  • Akhirah.
  • Jannah and Jahannam.
  • Khulafa-e-Rashideen.
  • Islamic history.
  • The purpose and mission of Islam.

★ Stage 2 for Teenagers, Adults and Reverts

This track is for teenagers, youth, adults and reverts. It is also for students who have completed the kids track and are now ready for deeper and more realistic visual learning.

This stage is especially important for those who were born Muslim but never built a deep internal connection with Islam. Many young people may know the Kalimah, but they may still carry many questions in their hearts. If these questions are ignored, they may slowly move away from Deen.

This stage is designed to rebuild clarity, interest, Iman and purpose.

Recommended Method for Stage 3

Students should maintain a notebook for this stage. Recommended method:

1
Listen to the Seerah lecture or audiobook.
2
Read the same portion from the Seerah book.
3
Write notes in a notebook.
4
Mention what was studied.
5
Mention what was understood.
6
Mention key lessons from the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
7
Discuss important points with the teacher or supervisor.
This stage should ideally be completed in 2 to 3 months, depending on the daily time available.
Stage 3 Outcome
  • Have a deeper understanding of Seerah.
  • Know the major events of the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
  • Understand the struggle and mission of Islam.
  • Have personal notes from Seerah.
  • Be ready to understand Quran and Salah more deeply in Stage 4.

Basic Islamic Laws and Systems of Islam
Teenagers Adults Reverts
▼

This stage introduces the student to Islam as a complete way of life. The student should learn the basic Islamic guidance needed for daily life and the broader systems of Islam.

5.1
Basic Islamic Laws and Daily Life Guidance
▼

This section includes the basic and essential Islamic terms, rules and laws needed for daily life. Topics include:

  • Taharah (purification).
  • Salah (prayer).
  • Halal and Haram.
  • Family responsibilities.
  • Rights of parents.
  • Rights of neighbors.
  • Rights of Muslims.
  • Akhlaq (character and manners).
  • Daily life Islamic rules.
  • Basic Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence).
  • Essential Ahkam (Islamic rulings).

5.2
Islamic Systems and Khilafah
▼

This section is designed to help students understand Islam as a complete system of life. Topics include:

  • Shariah (Islamic law as a complete framework).
  • Islamic political system.
  • Islamic economic system.
  • Islamic social system.
  • Islamic education system.
  • Islamic judicial system.
  • Concept of Khilafah.
  • Role of the Khalifah.
  • Role of Shura (consultation council).
  • Role of the general public.
  • Role of the army.
  • Role of institutions under Islamic governance.
  • Responsibilities of Muslims within the Ummah.
At the moment, United Ummah does not yet have one complete, reliable and properly structured playlist or course for this full section. Until such a course is available, this stage can be handled through:

• Research-based tasks
• Teacher-guided discussions
• Scholar-reviewed notes
• Selected readings
• Controlled study sessions
• Future course development

United Ummah intends to continue searching for suitable resources. If needed, United Ummah may contact course makers, teachers and scholars to help develop a proper course for this stage.
Important Note for Stage 5: Stage 5 should be taught with wisdom, balance and scholarly guidance. Fiqh, Islamic systems and Khilafah-related topics must be handled carefully so that students develop clarity, responsibility and understanding rather than confusion.
Stage 5 Outcome
  • Understand basic Islamic daily life rules.
  • Understand essential Islamic terminology.
  • Understand Islam as a complete way of life.
  • Understand the basic concept of Khilafah.
  • Understand the basic roles of Khalifah, Shura, public, army and institutions.
  • Understand that Islam guides worship, family, society, economy, justice and governance.
  • Be ready for lifelong advanced Islamic learning.

Recommended areas of continued learning include:

📚
Hadith

⚖️
Fiqh (Advanced)
Deep study of Islamic jurisprudence and the rulings of the Shariah.
🔤
Advanced Arabic
Study of the Arabic language to understand the Quran and Islamic texts directly.
🌍
Islamic History
In-depth study of Islamic civilization, dynasties, scholars and their contributions.
📢
Dawah
Learning the methods, etiquettes and responsibilities of calling people to Islam.
💎
Tazkiyah
Purification of the soul, spiritual development and closeness to Allah.
🏛️
Islamic Civilization
Study of the contributions and achievements of Islamic civilization across history.
🌐
Contemporary Challenges
Understanding modern challenges and how Islam provides guidance for them.
🤝
Service to the Ummah
Active engagement in the welfare, development and revival of the Muslim Ummah.
Recommended Starting Resources
  • Al-Bidaya Wal-Nihaya (The Beginning and the End) — comprehensive Islamic history.
  • Tafheem-ul-Quran — Mawdudi's detailed commentary on the Quran.
  • Advanced Arabic — to read primary Islamic texts directly.
  • Hadith and Fiqh — study of Islamic rulings from authentic sources.
These can be studied gradually and in parallel according to the student's capacity, teacher guidance and available time. The goal is to keep learning, implementing and teaching others.

A Muslim's journey of knowledge does not end with one course or one curriculum. This curriculum builds the foundation. After that, the student should continue learning throughout life.
